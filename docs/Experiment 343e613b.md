# By experiment we mean physical experiment

<br>

There are 2 EDDS devices: passive and active. The mouthpiece of the active device is connected to the air channel of the passive device by a tube. The active device generates vapor and sends it to the passive device. The vapor goes through the passive device and exits at the mouthpiece and into open space. The goal of the validation experiment is to measure temperature at the mouthpiece and airflow exiting the mouthpiece. ​

Phenomena Captured:​

- Single phase airflow velocity at the mouthpiece​
- Heated airflow at the mouthpiece​

Experiments: ​

- Devices: Passive and Active ​
- Bench testing measuring heated particles temperature at the mouth piece using thermocouple. We can heat up the particles using low powers (avoid burning wick) while they are passing through the atomizer.​
- Bench testing measuring heated particles velocity at the mouth piece using PIV.​
- We can stop/allow air passing through the wick
- Power Applied: 1 Watt, 5 Watts
- Airflow: 0.5 L/min, 0.75 L/min, 1 L/min, 1.5 L/min  

Experimental Variability in Passive Device: ​

- Environment: Humidity & Temperature ​
- Position of vent (funnel)​
- Sealing of passive device​
- PIV measurement ​
- Airflow from active to passive due to particles​
- Thermocouple placement​
- Manufacturing variability of experimental design parameters​
